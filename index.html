About Us

The Halal Food Foundation (HFF) is a UK-registered charity (1139457) dedicated to promoting the understanding and accessibility of halal principles. We believe halal is more than dietary rules—it's a holistic lifestyle encompassing health, cleanliness, and ethical consumption.

Through education, training, and community engagement, we empower individuals and organizations to make informed choices about halal living.

Our Vision & Mission

Founded with a vision to bridge knowledge gaps in the Muslim community and beyond, HFF operates on the principle: "Give a man a fish, feed him for a day; teach a man to fish, feed him for a lifetime." We believe education is key to fostering appreciation for halal's abundant benefits.

What We Do

We address critical gaps in halal education through:

  • Comprehensive Training Programs: Workshops and seminars for mosques, schools, and Islamic centers
  • Educational Resources: Developing accessible materials on halal principles and practices
  • Community Outreach: Engaging diverse audiences to promote halal awareness
  • Industry Collaboration: Partnering with halal producers and certifiers

Born from Halal Accreditation Limited's vision in 2013, HFF serves as an educational arm, helping communities understand not just what is halal, but why it matters—from food selection to lifestyle choices.

Why It Matters

Maintaining a halal lifestyle requires discipline, but with the growing halal industry, accessing halal products has never been easier. We help people navigate this landscape while understanding halal's profound health, ethical, and spiritual dimensions.
